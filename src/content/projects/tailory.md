Tailory is a client-side resume editor that lets you upload an existing resume, edit the content, preview changes in real time, and export the final result as a polished PDF. Everything runs locally in your browser — no accounts, no servers, and no data leaves your machine.

Built with SolidJS and styled with Tailwind CSS, Tailory provides a smooth and responsive editing experience. The PDF generation is powered by pdfmake, giving you precise control over the layout and styling of your exported resume.

## Features

- Upload and parse existing resumes for quick edits
- Live preview as you edit sections, skills, and experience
- One-click PDF export with professional formatting
