Reshrimp is a client-side image manipulation tool that runs entirely in the browser. It lets you resize, compress, and convert image formats without uploading files to any server, keeping your data private and local.

Built with modern web technologies, Reshrimp offers a fast and intuitive interface for quick image editing tasks. Drag and drop your images, tweak the settings, and download the result instantly.

## Features

- Resize images to custom dimensions or preset sizes
- Compress images with adjustable quality settings
- Convert between popular image formats including PNG, JPEG, and WebP
