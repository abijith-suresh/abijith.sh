Microbreak offers a collection of short, non-addictive puzzle games designed to fill those brief moments during build wait times. Instead of doom-scrolling, spend a couple of minutes solving a quick puzzle and get back to work refreshed.

Each game is intentionally bite-sized, with no progression systems, infinite scrolling, or engagement traps. The goal is a clean mental reset, not another time sink.

## Features

- Quick puzzle games that finish in under two minutes
- No accounts, no scores, no notifications
- Clean, minimal interface with no distractions
